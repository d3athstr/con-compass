  NET /BATT_GND    J2.2  U1.BAT-          (common with /GND)

  NET /SDA         J3.3  J4.4  U1.5(D4/SDA)
  NET /SCL         J3.4  J4.3  U1.6(D5/SCL)

  NET /HAPTIC_G    Q1.1(G)  R1.1  U1.4(D3/A3= GPIO4)
  NET /HAPTIC_M    J5.2  Q1.3(D)  D1.A
  NET /BTN_CYCLE   SW2.1  U1.2(D1/A1 = GPIO2)
  NET /BTN_PING    SW3.1  U1.3(D2/A2 = GPIO3)
  NET /BTN_HOME    SW4.1  U1.7(D6 = GPIO43)

  Buttons use the ESP32 internal pull-ups and short to GND when pressed.
  No external pull-up parts.

  I2C PULL-UPS: none on this carrier, on purpose. Both I2C devices are
  breakout modules (DS1 and U3) that carry their own. Adding a third pair
  here would put three sets in parallel and over-stiffen the bus.


U1 PIN BUDGET - RESOLVED 2026-09-07, it fits with room to spare

  Castellated pins CONSUMED by the Wio-SX1262 (SPI only):
    U1.9(D8  = GPIO7)  LORA_SCK
    U1.10(D9 = GPIO8)  LORA_MISO
    U1.11(D10= GPIO9)  LORA_MOSI

  The SX1262's control lines do NOT cost a castellated pin. They sit on GPIOs
  that the XIAO ESP32-S3 does not bring out to the 14-pin header at all - they
  exist only on the underside B2B connector:
    CS=GPIO41  RESET=GPIO42  DIO1=GPIO39  BUSY=GPIO40  RXEN=GPIO38
  Source: meshtastic/firmware variants/esp32s3/seeed_xiao_s3/variant.h.
  That variant also fixes I2C at SDA=GPIO5 (D4) and SCL=GPIO6 (D5), which is
  exactly the assignment used above - keep it, so a badge can be flashed with
  stock Meshtastic for debugging without rewiring.

  UNCONNECTED / FREE
    U1.14(5V)      - USB 5V, unused; charging is internal to the XIAO
    U1.1(D0/A0  = GPIO1)  free. Avoided on purpose: stock Meshtastic drives
                          it as PIN_GPS_STANDBY. Do not hang the haptic here.
    U1.8(D7 = GPIO44)     free. Avoided for the haptic for the same reason -
                          stock Meshtastic uses GPIO43/44 as the GPS UART, and
                          a UART idles HIGH, which would run the motor
                          continuously. SW4 sits on GPIO43 instead, which is
                          harmless: it is an input, and a driven pin simply
                          reads driven.
    The haptic gate is on D3/GPIO4, which the variant leaves unassigned - so
    the motor cannot be woken by a stray peripheral under any firmware.


THE U1 <-> U2 B2B INTERFACE (not routed here, but it constrains this board)

  The Wio-SX1262 does NOT sit in its own XIAO land. It mates to the XIAO
  ESP32-S3's underside board-to-board connector and sits directly beneath U1
  - the same arrangement Sentinel uses ("the Wio-SX1262 rides on top of U2 and
  is off this board", techtaria-sentinel/README.md:108).

  Three consequences this carrier MUST honour:

  1. MECHANICAL. The area under U1's XIAO land must be a KEEPOUT with a
     clearance cutout: no copper, no silk, no components, and enough Z for the
     module. U1 sits on J1 socket strips, so the stack height is
     socket + Wio-SX1262 + XIAO. Measure the real stack before fixing the
     shell depth.
  2. ANTENNA. AE1, the 915MHz u.FL, lands on the Wio-SX1262 itself, NOT on
     this board. Its lead has to escape the cutout and reach the top edge.
  3. ELECTRICAL. No SPI, NSS, BUSY, RST or DIO net appears on this carrier.
     Of the pins those signals use, only the SPI trio (D8/D9/D10) is also a
     castellated pin and therefore spent; CS/RST/DIO1/BUSY/RXEN are B2B-only
     and cost this board nothing. See U1 PIN BUDGET above.

  Firmware note: this exact combo is an official Meshtastic target,
  variant esp32s3/seeed_xiao_s3.


POLARITY / ORIENTATION - the class of error that passes DRC
  D1   1N4148 flyback. BANDED END = CATHODE = to /+3V3. Reversed, it shorts
       the 3V3 rail through Q1 every time the motor is driven.
  Q1   AO3400 SOT-23: pin 1 GATE, pin 2 SOURCE (to GND), pin 3 DRAIN (to M1).
  C1   ceramic disc, non-polarised, orientation free.
  C2   ceramic disc, non-polarised, orientation free.
  BT1  LiPo. Confirm the JST PH polarity against the CELL, not against the
       connector shell - cheap LiPo leads are not consistently wired, and the
       XIAO's BAT pads will not survive a reversal.
  U3   MAX17048 VIN IS LEFT OPEN. The die is cell-derived. Strapping VIN to
       3V3 backfeeds the 3V3 rail and defeats SW1 - the badge will not turn
       off. This is the exact fault found on PipBoy 2026-08-20.


LANDS - the check that DRC cannot do

  J1 XIAO_THT land: hole columns 15.24 mm apart.
  NOT 17.0 mm. 17.0 mm is the SMD CASTELLATION span from Seeed's datasheet.
  Source: Seeed "XIAO Series Package and PCB Design" p.7, confirmed against
  Seeed's own XIAO-ESP32-C6-DIP.kicad_mod.
  Getting this wrong scrapped 25 fabbed boards across six carriers on
  2026-08-29. Every route and fab run must pass kicad_land_gate.py.


POWER PATH

  USB-C (on U1) ---> XIAO internal charger ---> BT1
                                                 (charge current is the
                                                  XIAO's own, ~100mA)

  BT1 3.0-4.2V
    |
  J2.1 /VBAT_RAW
    |
  SW1  slide switch          <-- HARD ISOLATION. Deep sleep alone is not
    |                            enough between events; these sit in a
    |                            drawer for a year at a time.
    |
  +-- /VBAT_SW --+-- U1.BAT+     (XIAO powers itself + charges from here)
                 |
                 +-- J3.1        (U3 MAX17048 senses the CELL directly,
                                  NOT the 3V3 rail - a gauge reading a
                                  regulated rail reads a flat line and
                                  tells you nothing)

  U1.12 /+3V3  (XIAO's own LDO, ~700mA available)
    |
    +-- C1 0.1uF --- GND         bulk near the B2B / LoRa supply
    +-- C2 0.1uF --- GND         bulk at the I2C rail
    +-- J4.2                     DS1 OLED
    +-- J5.1 --------------------+
                                 |
                                 M1  coin motor, 2.0-3.5V, ~75mA
                                 |
    /+3V3 --- D1 (K to rail) ----+  <-- FLYBACK. M1 is inductive.
                                 |      Without D1, Q1 dies.
                                 |
                            Q1.3 (DRAIN)
                                 |
                            Q1 AO3400  <-- Q1.1 GATE
                                 |            |
                            Q1.2 (SOURCE)     +-- R1 10k --- GND
                                 |            |
                                GND           +-- U1.D6
                                                  R1 holds M1 OFF while the
                                                  GPIO is high-Z during boot
                                                  and reset. Without it the
                                                  badge buzzes on every
                                                  power-up.

  NOTE: M1 runs from the REGULATED 3V3 rail, not the raw cell. The motor is
  rated 2.0-3.5V and a full LiPo at 4.2V would overdrive it.

  POWER BUDGET (the real constraint: 16 hours off an outlet on 1200mAh)
    Nothing here is continuous-duty. The design only closes if:
      - DS1 is DARK unless a button was pressed  (OLED is the biggest drain)
      - LoRa RX duty-cycles between beacons
      - BLE advertises slowly until homing mode is entered
      - M1 is PULSED, never held (~75mA while running)
    Homing mode is deliberately the expensive mode - full-rate BLE scan and
    fast LoRa polling - entered by SW4 and timed out automatically.

OPEN ITEMS

  1. [RESOLVED 2026-09-07] GPIO budget vs the B2B. It fits, with two pins
     spare - see U1 PIN BUDGET above. The SX1262's control lines live on
     GPIO38-42, which are not on the castellated header, so only the SPI trio
     is spent. The resistor-ladder fallback is NOT needed and no ladder
     resistors belong on the BOM.

  2. ANTENNA PLACEMENT ON A BODY-WORN BADGE. *** BLOCKS LAYOUT ***
     A wearer is a bag of saltwater and absorbs a great deal of 915MHz. AE1
     must run along the TOP EDGE, outside the shell's board tray, with no
     ground pour, battery or metal insert between it and the outside world.
     This wants measuring on a prototype before the outline is fixed, not
     assuming.

  3. (Not blocking) Whether SW2 should be a rotary encoder instead, for
     scrolling a 10-person peer list. Three buttons may be too few.
